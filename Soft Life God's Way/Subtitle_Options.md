## Editorial note on option 3

The source's own definition says this life does **not** eliminate all difficulty, while option 3 promises "stress-free." That is a tension a Christian reader will notice. The option is preserved exactly as supplied; if it is ever used publicly, it should be paired with the book's own framing that biblical peace is not the absence of trials (John 16:33; 2 Corinthians 4:8–9).
