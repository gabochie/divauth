## What was deliberately not carried over

- **Unverified named attributions**: "As Jasmine Rutledge outlines…", "As She Ministries highlights…", Facebook and Instagram post titles, "Pastor Olumide Emmanuel", "Daniela The Brand", "Living With Love", "Growing Through It", "Calm". These were search-result snippets, not checked sources. The book cites Scripture and standard Christian teaching instead.
- **Retrieval artefacts**: view counts, upload ages, video durations, "YouTube ·", "Facebook·", "Instagram·", and source URLs with tracking parameters.
- **Chat artefacts**: "Would you like to focus on…?", "Use the highlighted definition to creat[…]", "Here are a few direct-response sub-headline options…", "I would go with the first option…".
- **The promise "stress-free"** from subtitle option 3. It is preserved verbatim in `Subtitle_Options.md` but is not used as the book's subtitle: the source's own definition denies a trouble-free life, and Scripture does too (John 16:33).

## Safeguard decisions

The source's emphasis on "ease" and "stress reduction" could drift into prosperity teaching. The manuscript therefore states repeatedly:

- biblical rest is not laziness, avoidance of suffering, or a guarantee of comfort;
- softness includes boundaries, emotional healing and purpose;
- prayer and spiritual rhythms coexist with medical and mental-health care;
- a woman in danger should seek safety and proper reporting, not a lesson in gentleness.
