## Non-negotiables for every product

These come from the book's own safeguarding section and must survive into anything built from it:

- **No diagnosis.** The assessment must use "pattern", never "you have X" or "this is demonic".
- **No medical claims.** Rest tools must not promise to treat anxiety, depression, burnout or illness. Prayer and qualified care coexist.
- **No "stress-free" promise.** Subtitle option 3 was rejected for this reason (John 16:33); do not reintroduce it in marketing copy.
- **Abuse referral.** Every boundary or inner-peace tool needs a visible "if you are in danger, seek help first" path.
- **Privacy.** Any rest/mood journal app keeps data local or uses clear consent; it is not a clinical record.
