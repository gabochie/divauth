A Christ-Centred Theology and Practice
The King is not merely stronger.
He is Lord.

Jesus did not negotiate with demons or depend on their cooperation. He commanded, and they obeyed. Divine Authority recovers that vision for the church today: a deliverance ministry that begins with the identity of Christ, not the activity of the enemy.

Drawing on the Gospels, the Epistles, and the original biblical languages, this book joins careful theology to sober pastoral practice.

· A biblical theology of authority, power, and dominion
· Seven encounters with demons and unclean spirits
· A safe, ethical, person-centred ministry protocol
· A complete ten-lesson teaching curriculum
· Forms, checklists, aftercare plans, and study questions
